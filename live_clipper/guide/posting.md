# Posting

You choose the destinations in the wizard: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or none.

## Manual mode

Each clip waits for you. Review it, edit it if you like, then post it to the destination you want.
Nothing leaves your computer until you do.

## Automatic mode

A clip posts to your chosen destinations as soon as it is ready. Use it only for channels you own or have
permission to post from. You can switch back to manual mode at any time, and clips already waiting stay
waiting.

Storage cleanup never deletes clips that are queued, held or uploading, or clips that have not been posted.
